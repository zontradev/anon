Privacy Policy

Price Lister is engineered under a formal corporate privacy standard. Across both our Website Console (https://pricelister-xi.vercel.app/) and our Android application architecture, we require strictly Camera and Internet access. We never access or harvest device contacts, SMS logs, GPS coordinates, or microphone audio.

Firebase Authentication & Analytics

User authentication is managed via Google Firebase Authentication using encrypted JWT session tokens. Firebase Analytics is utilized strictly to monitor application stability, crash-free rates, and aggregate performance metrics without selling or sharing personal business data.

Supabase Database & Storage

All product price lists, Customer Invoices (B2C), Business Invoices (B2B), and client records are isolated per account using Supabase Row-Level Security (RLS) policies and stored locally in a high-speed SQLite database for offline resilience.
